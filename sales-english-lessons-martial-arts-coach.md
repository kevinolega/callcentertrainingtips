# THREE LEVELS OF PRACTICE

You already know how martial arts training works.

You don't become good at a technique by watching someone demonstrate it once.

You need to practice.

You need feedback.

And eventually, you need to test your skills under pressure.

Communication follows the same principle.

## LEVEL 1: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU START TO GET IT RIGHT.

In martial arts, you learn techniques with cooperative partners.

You practice slowly.

Your coach corrects your mistakes.

In communication coaching, we'll practice:

- Building clear English sentences.
- Explaining simple techniques.
- Introducing yourself.
- Describing your qualifications.
- Answering basic membership questions.
- Discussing your private coaching packages.

We'll use examples, scripts, and guided exercises.

**Your goal is to start communicating correctly, even when you still need help.**

## LEVEL 2: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU GET IT RIGHT CONSISTENTLY.

In martial arts, you repeat techniques with different partners.

You practice with movement and controlled resistance.

In communication coaching, we'll practice:

- Explaining techniques without scripts.
- Answering different student questions.
- Presenting different coaching packages.
- Responding to membership inquiries.
- Discussing your rates.
- Handling common objections.
- Explaining your experience in your own words.

**Your goal is to communicate well across different situations.**

## LEVEL 3: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU CAN'T GET IT WRONG.

In martial arts, you test your techniques against opponents who actively resist.

You need to adjust under pressure.

In communication coaching, we'll practice:

- Difficult sales conversations.
- Unexpected student questions.
- Clients who challenge your prices.
- International coaching interviews.
- Negotiations.
- Teaching demonstrations.
- Complaints.
- Conversations with potential sponsors and partners.

**Your goal is to handle difficult conversations without losing your confidence.**
