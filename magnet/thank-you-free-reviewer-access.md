## STEP 2: START YOUR 30-DAY CHALLENGE

**Give me 30 days of serious effort, and I guarantee you will get your dream call center job.**

Over the years, many readers and students have contacted me to say they got hired after studying my lessons and practicing what they learned.

Some joined my paid training sessions.

Others studied my free lessons, watched my videos, and practiced on their own.

I've seen this happen over and over again.

But you have to do the work.

Here's what I want you to do:

1. Read and study the reviewer.
2. Complete the exercises.
3. Practice writing English sentences every day.
4. Practice speaking your answers aloud.
5. Review your mistakes and correct them.
6. Prepare for common and difficult interview questions.
7. Apply for jobs and learn from your interviews.

You don't have to get everything right immediately.

The goal is to identify your mistakes, correct them, and improve through practice.

**Don't just collect training materials. Use them.**

### THREE LEVELS OF PRACTICE

I recommend following three levels of practice.

**LEVEL 1: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU START TO GET IT RIGHT.**

Study the examples.

Learn the correct answers.

Practice basic English sentences and interview questions.

**LEVEL 2: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU GET IT RIGHT CONSISTENTLY.**

Practice answering questions without reading scripts.

Correct mistakes that keep coming back.

Explain your ideas in your own words.

**LEVEL 3: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU CAN'T GET IT WRONG.**

Practice unexpected interview questions.

Prepare for difficult conversations.

Learn to recover when you forget an answer or make a mistake.

**The goal isn't just to finish reading the reviewer. The goal is to become someone who can handle the interview.**
