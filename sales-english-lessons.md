<h2>PRIVATE ENGLISH AND INTERVIEW COACHING</h2>

<p>Do you struggle to explain your ideas in English, answer interview questions, or speak confidently during professional conversations?</p>

<p>Private coaching gives you individual attention, practical exercises, and direct feedback on your communication skills.</p>

<p>Instead of following a large class, we'll focus on your specific problems and work on improving them together.</p>

<h2>WHO IS THIS FOR?</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Call center applicants preparing for job interviews.</li>
  <li>People who struggle to express their ideas in English.</li>
  <li>Students who want personal grammar and pronunciation corrections.</li>
  <li>Shy or introverted applicants who need speaking practice.</li>
  <li>Freelancers and professionals who want to communicate more effectively.</li>
  <li>Students who cannot attend our regular group training schedule.</li>
</ul>

<h2>WHAT CAN WE WORK ON?</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Grammar and sentence building.</li>
  <li>English pronunciation and commonly used phrases.</li>
  <li>Introducing yourself during interviews.</li>
  <li>Answering interview questions with clear examples.</li>
  <li>Professional conversations and customer communication.</li>
  <li>Mock interviews and call handling practice.</li>
  <li>Identifying communication problems and building a practice plan.</li>
</ul>

<h2>FOUR-SESSION TRAINING PROGRAM</h2>

<p>Our four-session package provides a structured introduction to communication skills and pronunciation practice.</p>

<h3>Session 1: Goal Setting and Communication Skills 1</h3>

<p>We'll identify your goals, discuss your communication difficulties, and begin exercises to improve how you express your ideas.</p>

<h3>Session 2: Communication Skills 2</h3>

<p>Practice sentence building, storytelling, explaining experiences, and answering professional or interview questions.</p>

<h3>Session 3: Pronunciation 1</h3>

<p>Practice common English words and professional phrases. We'll identify pronunciation problems and work on correcting them.</p>

<h3>Session 4: Pronunciation 2</h3>

<p>Continue pronunciation exercises, review your progress, and identify areas that need more practice.</p>

<p><strong>Each session lasts 60 minutes.</strong></p>

<p>The training plan may be adjusted based on your needs. Some students need more communication practice, while others need more pronunciation work.</p>

<h2>HOW DOES PRIVATE COACHING WORK?</h2>

<ol>
  <li>We'll discuss your goals and identify the communication skills you need to improve.</li>
  <li>You'll participate in speaking, writing, and pronunciation exercises.</li>

<p>Improvement depends on your participation and consistent practice. Private coaching provides guidance and feedback, but you still need to do the work.</p>
