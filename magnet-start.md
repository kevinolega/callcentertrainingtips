## STEP 6. START APPLYING FOR CALL CENTER JOBS

**YOU DON'T NEED TO FINISH EVERY LESSON BEFORE APPLYING.**

Don't spend six months watching videos without sending a single application.

Start applying when you're ready to attempt interviews.

An interview gives you the chance to test what you've learned.

If you pass, continue with the hiring process.

If you fail, review what happened.

Ask yourself:

- Which questions did I struggle to answer?
- Did I understand the interviewer?
- Did I explain my experience clearly?
- Did I make grammar mistakes?
- Did I struggle with pronunciation?
- Did I freeze or forget my answers?
- Did I have trouble answering follow-up questions?
- What should I practice before my next interview?

Write down the problems you remember.

Find the lessons that address them.

Practice.

Then apply again.

**Don't make the same mistake ten times without trying to correct it.**

Each interview should help you identify something you can improve.

# YOUR 30-DAY JOB APPLICATION CHALLENGE

**Give me 30 days of serious effort, and I guarantee you will get your dream call center job.**

You can begin this challenge with the free resources above.

Here's what I want you to do.

## EVERY DAY

1. Read or watch lessons that address your weak areas.
2. Practice speaking English.
3. Complete writing exercises.
4. Review mistakes you identified.
5. Practice answering interview questions.
6. Prepare for or submit job applications.

When you receive interview invitations, attend them.

When you fail, review what happened.

Then correct your mistakes and try again.

### DON'T JUST MEMORIZE ANSWERS.

Interviewers won't always ask the exact questions you studied.

They may interrupt you.

They may ask you to explain an answer.

They may ask something unexpected.

**You need to practice thinking, responding, and explaining your ideas in English.**

### DON'T WAIT UNTIL YOU FEEL PERFECT.

You won't know how you'll perform until you try.

Apply for jobs.

Attend interviews.

Learn from the results.

Keep improving.

### DON'T IGNORE YOUR MISTAKES.

Some mistakes will be easy to correct.

Others will take repeated practice.

Some may return when you're nervous.

That's why you need to review your performance.

**Identify the mistake. Correct it. Practice. Test again.**

That's the same method I use in my paid training sessions.
