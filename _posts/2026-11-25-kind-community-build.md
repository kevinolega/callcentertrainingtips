I've spent years creating free lessons, reviewers, videos, and other resources to help people improve their skills and find better opportunities.

I want you to use them.

I'm not expecting everyone to have perfect English, a good education, money, or previous work experience.

I'm looking for people who are willing to learn and take action.

Read the lessons. Follow the instructions. Try the exercises. Apply for jobs. Make mistakes. Learn from them.

If you need help, show me what you've already tried.

I'm happy to answer questions during group sessions and scheduled office hours. I may also volunteer some personal time for students who complete the activities and demonstrate effort.

But I can't spend hours convincing people to study, repeatedly explaining information that's already available, or chasing people who aren't taking action.

I also want you to stay in touch.

Tell me when you get hired, pass training, receive your first paycheck, get promoted, find a freelance client, or start a business.

Tell me what worked and what didn't.

I'd like this to become a community of people who study, work, improve their skills, and help one another.

You don't need to be successful to belong here.

You need to be willing to do the work.
