I've spent years creating free lessons, reviewers, videos, and training materials to help people find jobs, improve their skills, and increase their income.

I want people to use these resources.

I don't expect everyone to have perfect English, a college degree, money, confidence, or previous work experience.

I don't expect everyone to succeed on their first attempt.

**I'm looking for people who are willing to learn and take action.**

## What I Expect From Students

1. **Read the lessons and instructions.** I've already spent time preparing them so you can study without waiting for me.
2. **Try the exercises.** You don't need to get everything right. I want you to practice.
3. **Show me what you've done.** If you want feedback, share your answers, recordings, applications, or completed activities.
4. **Ask specific questions.** Tell me what you're trying to accomplish, what you've already tried, and where you're having trouble.
5. **Take responsibility for your progress.** I can teach you, but I cannot study, practice, or apply for jobs on your behalf.
6. **Respect everyone's time.** Come prepared to group sessions and help other students when you can.

## How You Can Get More Help

I'm preparing a simple self-study system.

You'll have opportunities to:

- Set a goal and prepare an action plan.
- Complete practical activities using the free lessons.
- Take a final assessment to demonstrate what you've learned.

You don't need perfect answers.

I'm interested in your effort, improvement, and willingness to follow instructions.

Students who complete the activities may receive additional feedback or an invitation to a discussion when my schedule allows.

I'll also continue offering group sessions and occasional office hours.

Personal coaching and regular individual feedback are paid services.

## What I Cannot Do

I cannot spend hours convincing someone to study.

I cannot repeatedly explain lessons that are already available on the website.

I cannot chase students who stop participating.

I cannot promise unlimited private messages, free consultations, or individual coaching.

I understand that people get discouraged, make mistakes, and face difficult circumstances.

You can struggle and still be a good student.

But I cannot want your success more than you do.

## Please Stay in Touch

I want to hear from you after you use the lessons.

Tell me when you get hired.

Tell me when you pass training, receive your first paycheck, get regularized, or get promoted.

Tell me when you find your first freelance client or start your own business.

Tell me about your mistakes and what you've learned.

You don't have to wait until you've achieved something big.

If you've completed an activity, improved an interview answer, or finally understood something that confused you, I'd like to hear about it.

Your progress helps me understand which lessons are useful. Your experiences may also help other students.

## What I Want This Community to Become

I don't want a community where everyone waits for me to tell them what to do.

I want people who study, practice, share their progress, ask useful questions, and help one another.

Some of you may eventually join paid coaching sessions, purchase books, or support my work in other ways.

That's appreciated, but you don't have to buy anything to use the free resources.

I want to build lasting relationships with people who appreciate the opportunity and make good use of it.

**You don't need to be successful to belong here.**

**You need to be willing to do the work.**

And when you make progress, I'd like to hear about it.
