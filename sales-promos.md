## 4. HOW MY TRAINING METHOD WORKS

**I DON'T BELIEVE YOU NEED MORE RANDOM ENGLISH TIPS.**

You need someone who can identify the mistakes you keep making.

That's why I use the same four-step correction process across my coaching programs.

### STEP 1: IDENTIFY YOUR MISTAKES

We'll discuss your goals and review your communication problems.

I'll identify the mistakes that affect your interviews, work, clients, or business.

### STEP 2: EXPLAIN THE CORRECTIONS

I'll explain what went wrong and show you how to improve.

We'll practice the correct sentences, responses, questions, and communication habits.

### STEP 3: ASSIGN PRACTICE EXERCISES

You'll receive exercises based on your problems.

These may include writing activities, speaking practice, mock interviews, sales roleplays, or workplace conversations.

### STEP 4: TEST AGAIN

We'll review your work and check whether you've improved.

If you keep making the same mistake, we'll work on it again.

If you've corrected it, we'll move to another problem.

**IDENTIFY THE MISTAKE. CORRECT IT. PRACTICE. TEST AGAIN.**

### THREE LEVELS OF PRACTICE

I use the same training principles in martial arts and communication coaching.

**LEVEL 1: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU START TO GET IT RIGHT.**

Learn the correct approach.

Use examples, scripts, and guided exercises.

**LEVEL 2: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU GET IT RIGHT CONSISTENTLY.**

Practice different situations.

Reduce your dependence on scripts.

Correct mistakes that keep coming back.

**LEVEL 3: PRACTICE UNTIL YOU CAN'T GET IT WRONG.**

Practice difficult conversations.

Respond to unexpected questions.

Learn to communicate under pressure.

The lessons and exercises will depend on your goals.
